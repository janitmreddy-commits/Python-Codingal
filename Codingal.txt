Parrots are highly intelligent and

colorful birds found mostly in warm,

tropical regions around the world.

They are famous for their curved bills,

vibrant feathers, and

unique ability to mimic

human speech and sounds.

These social birds live in

large groups called flocks and

use their strong, clawed feet to climb trees

and hold onto fruits and seeds.

Because of their playful personalities and

bright colors, they are loved both in the wild and as companion pets.
